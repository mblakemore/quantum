One dial for the arrow of time.

There is a knob on this experiment and it controls exactly one thing: how much the environment remembers of what happened to a qubit. That single knob sets four physically distinct quantities at once, each by its own predicted law — how wave-like the system still is, how objective the fact has become, how strongly the two are linked, and how much energy a single classical bit can pour into the environment that did the recording. The unification is the result. The energy is the part that sounds impossible, and it is the illustration, not the claim.

Room 1 — Three faces, one knob

The knob is θ, and κ = cos(θ/2) is how strongly the bath recorded the system (that mapping is the flight's own, from its committed manifest, not a convention chosen for this page). At κ = 1 nothing was recorded and the system is still a clean wave. At κ = 0 the record is complete and the wave is gone. The claim is not that these three quantities are correlated — it is that each is a specific function of the same κ, predicted in advance, and the chip drew all three.

AUDIT — all three registered gates held. The measured points sit on their predicted laws without fitting: the dashed curves are κ and κ² as written down before the flight, not regressions through the data. All three faces come from one circuit per setting — the system measured in X and the bath in Z gives every correlator at once, so no face can drift relative to another through a separate calibration.

Room 2 — And energy rides the same knob

Exp227 G4 · the same apparatus, plus a bath field and one classical bit

Reported, NOT certified — outside the registered verdict Everything in this room sits outside the registered verdict. The flight's pre-registered criteria were the three faces in room 1; this energy measurement was pre-registered as a reported headline with its hardware cost priced in advance, deliberately kept out of the pass/fail. That is visible in the record itself: the decode file carries g1, g2, g3 and a verdict flag, and no g4 at all. It is here because it is the most interesting thing the apparatus does — not because it was certified, and the page should not let those blur.

Quantum energy teleportation. Alice measures her qubit and sends Bob one classical bit. Bob rotates his own qubit according to that bit and extracts energy from it — energy that was not locally available to him a moment earlier. The prediction is that the amount tracks 1 − κ²: the more completely the bath has recorded the event, the more energy a single bit can put into it. At full record the bath is a maximally mixed, locally useless fog — and that is exactly where one bit does the most.

What 0.846 does and does not mean The energy is not carried by the bit. This is the misreading the number invites, so it goes first, in the flight designer's own words: the measurement at S pays for and injects the energy; the classical bit only tells B which local operation unlocks its share. Nothing is transported, and nothing is free — the protocol converts measurement back-action plus one bit of communication into locally extractable work. A reader who gets the magnitude wrong has a small problem; a reader who thinks a bit delivered energy to a distant qubit has the physics wrong.

It is a normalized figure. 0.846 is of the maximum this protocol can extract — not 0.846 of the energy in any absolute sense, and not a fraction of some larger reservoir. The sentence that writes itself here only keeps its meaning with that clause attached, so it is attached.

And the shortfall is a priced cost that varies along the dial. The measured curve tracks the predicted shape but sits below it at every setting — 71%, 73%, 80% and 85% of ideal — a deficit of 15–29% across the dial, narrowing toward full record (29%, 27%, 20%, 15% as κ falls). The deficit shrinking with record strength is a real fact about the hardware, not a constant tax. Note which end 0.846 sits at: quoting "about 15% short" alongside it would be quoting the most flattering point on the very curve being illustrated. The cause is the feed-forward — Bob's rotation waits on Alice's bit travelling a real classical path and the qubits decohere meanwhile — and that cost was quantified from an earlier flight's measured penalty and written into this flight's pre-registration in advance. So this is a number measured through a channel whose price was known before the data existed: which is both why it can be reported as-is, and why it is not certified.

One more thing you can see on the chart: at zero record the measured curve starts a hair below zero, at −0.0065 where the law says exactly 0. That is noise at a point where there is nothing to extract, not a negative extraction — it is left visible rather than cropped away.

What this adds, and what it doesn't

The museum already has a page saying energy teleportation works. Beam the Power certifies that the effect exists — an informed bit moves Bob's energy where a coin flip does not, as a differential between gate-identical arms. This page is a different question on the same physics: not whether it happens, but what it costs in record strength — the dial law. Neither number derives from the other and neither replaces the other. One shows the effect exists; the other shows how it scales.

The claim, scoped What is certified here: three quantities that look like different physics — a wave's visibility, a fact's objectivity, and the correlation between a system and its environment — are each a predicted function of one recording strength, measured across five settings on one apparatus. What is reported: the energy a single classical bit can teleport into that environment rises as the record completes, tracking 1 − κ² in shape at 71–85% of its ideal level. What is not claimed: no energy came from nowhere — Alice's measurement pays for it up front — and nothing here travels faster than the bit does.

The court

how we know we're not fooling ourselves

THE LAWS WERE WRITTEN DOWN FIRST

κ, κ² and 1−κ² are not fits. They were derived and checked against an exact statevector simulation before the flight, then compared to hardware. A curve that matches a law chosen in advance is a different kind of evidence from a curve with a line drawn through it afterwards.

ONE CIRCUIT, THREE FACES

Each dial setting is a single circuit: measure the system in X, the bath in Z, and all three correlators fall out of the same shots. The faces cannot drift apart through separate calibrations, because there are no separate calibrations.

THE UNCERTIFIED PART IS MARKED AS SUCH

The energy result is reported, NOT certified, and its room says so before it shows a number. The decode record carries g1/g2/g3 and no g4 — the absence is mechanical, not editorial. Pricing a known hardware cost into a pre-registration and then reporting the result honestly is not the same as certifying it.

FENCES

Two qubits; five dial settings; one device; a bath field standing in for a real environment. This is a toy apparatus for a real law, not a thermodynamic engine. Exp227 · findings in the repo
