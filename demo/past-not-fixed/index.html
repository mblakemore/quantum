Five flights from the Heron 2026 campaign — one thesis

The Past Is Not Fixed.

On quantum hardware, "what happened" is not a settled thing waiting to be read. Five rooms, five certified results: entanglement between states that never coexisted, decided later · a toy universe where time is entanglement — with an off-switch · a qubit with no definite value between looks · two operations with no definite order, sorted after the fact · and that same no-definite-value wall crossed a second time, months later, by a different and deliberately cheaper protocol. Nothing ever signals backward — every room carries its own audit.

Room 5 — The Same Wall, Crossed Again

Exp229 · job d9epva4jeosc73fj2v4g

Room 3 is not the only K₃ on this page — and that is the point. Months after Exp186, the macrorealist wall was crossed a second time by a different, deliberately cheaper protocol: a single qubit precessing under Rx(π/3), read with plain projective mid-circuit measurements and zero two-qubit gates — the cheapest flight of the campaign. It measured K₃ = 1.429 ± 0.017, past the bound of 1 at 25σ. Neither result replaces the other, and they are not equally strong: Room 3 credits the mid-time value only from detectors that provably never fired, which is what closes the macrorealist "your measurement disturbed it" excuse at the standard level. Room 5 does not close it — a projective measurement genuinely disturbs the qubit, so the clumsiness loophole stays open here. What Room 5 adds is independence: a separate flight, a separate circuit, a separate σ, landing on the same side of the same wall.

AUDIT — this is a violable bound, not an algebraic identity: the initial state is |+⟩, so every first measurement is genuinely ~50/50, and K₃ carries real shot-noise variance — it can land under 1.5, and did (1.429, 95% of the quantum maximum, the rest a readout haircut). Validity was checked explicitly before decode, the lesson taken from a flight (Exp228) that was caught being tautological and was not certified. WHERE IT LED — the very next flight took this room's line ("the past between glances is not written") and asked whether a later choice can select which past was real: the delayed-choice eraser.

The court

how we know we're not fooling ourselves

PRE-REGISTERED, FALSIFIED-FIRST, RULE-AMENDED

Every room flew with criteria committed before decode and falsifier arms that landed on script (product sorts flat; decohered sorts stay on the equator; dephased K₃ under the bound). Two flights missed their own letter by hairs (0.008; 0.0004) — both NOT-HELD verdicts stand, both re-flights used rule-derived criteria committed pre-flight, both replicated stronger.

WHAT "RETRO" REALLY MEANS

Nothing propagates backward — the audits prove it in-data. The later choice selects which sorting of the already-written record is meaningful. That is Wheeler's delayed choice, Peres's swap, and Leggett–Garg's missing past, all drawn by one chip in one night. The record is re-sorted, never rewritten.

THE TOOLKIT PLAYED BOTH SIDES

The instruments came from plumbing: frame-steered sifting (built for relay keys) sorts the ensembles; the engineered echo (built to cure the window tax) protects the order-coherence — and in room 4 it made the LATE choice beat the early one (0.467 vs 0.345, 196% of the delayed-choice cost recovered). The live coins (mid-circuit QRNG + dynamic branches) closed the compiled-choice fence on rooms 1 and 4.

FENCES

One die; toy universes; Clifford laws; sifted ensembles with published relay outcomes; the live coins are on-chip QRNG (ordering, not space-like separation); Leggett–Garg's clumsiness addressed at the standard (negative-result) level in room 3, and left open in room 5, which uses plain projective measurement.
