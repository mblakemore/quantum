The Decoder Race
— a sealed answer, read through noise

A deep quantum circuit's single loudest output dies exponentially with depth — the campaign measured that wall and published window-closed. Then a re-read of the fold's own discarded data showed every shot still whispers the hidden 40-bit answer with a few typos. Average the whispers letter-by-letter and you can read what no single shot can say. Six sealed races later, that decoder read a cryptographically-committed answer exactly, in 3.82 s, against a frozen classical floor of 1,818 s — 476× at the harshest edge, graded by three independent AI seats.

Scope: one instance family · one die per race · best-known-solver engineering race, not a complexity theorem · supersedable-by-design (a faster classical solver retires the number) · F54's brute-force wall untouched

📜 SIX RACES — every fence forged from the previous miss

RACE 0Window-closed. The modal observable dies by depth × width — verdict published with the number to beat. Then the discarded calibration data revealed the whisper code (F120: per-bit information decays ~30× slower).
RACE 1Fold — endianness. The decoder worked; a bit-order convention crossed between the sealer and builder. Caught by the court in minutes; convention round-trips added to every later flight.
RACE 2Fold — one bit. Gate rung placed 20% past race depth by fold arithmetic; missed exact by a single bit whose trend showed it was shots-starved. Fix: gates AT race depth.
RACE 3Fold — bad wires. Two readout-defective qubits corrupted the gate. Fix: whole-chip readout calibration folded into the decoder's per-bit thresholds.
RACE 4Cap branch. Hygiene validated — exact blind recovery at record depth 217 — but excluding bad qubits cost +92 depth and the routing drew past the cap. Lesson: fix defects in the decoder, not the routing.
RACE 5Miss — the control experiment. Dropped the routing exclusion to test whether it was load-bearing. It was (the free t=0 self-test caught the dirty register at zero seal cost). The pre-registered miss bought the final fence.
RACE 6WIN. Clean register certified by the free self-test before the seal was risked; twin exact at race depth; the sealed 40-bit answer read exactly from 12,500 shots. All three court seats concur. F121.

Sealed-commitment protocol: one AI (Ember) generates the answer and commits SHA-256(answer‖salt) to the public repo before any circuit exists; a second (Whisper) builds, flies, and decodes blind — ŝ posted publicly before the seal opens; a third (Elder) grades against a classical band frozen days earlier. The win prints its own expiry: a classical solver beating 1,818 s on this family retires the entry. Numbers: specification sheet · WIN verdict · program closure.
