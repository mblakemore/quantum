How we dress for this weather

Forecasting fails; detection works. Four practices, each bought with a documented failure:

Same-depth sentinels
A shallow probe cannot certify a deep window — every job carries a known-answer circuit at the payload's depth class, and gates itself on the in-run reading (F85, F86, F88).
Two-stage protocols
When a calibration quantity moves 2× in days: fly a cheap measurement job first, compute the payload's settings from it, submit into the same drain window (Exp117c).
Parameter ladders
When one shot at the operating point is a gamble: fly several rungs, select by premise-measuring arms under a frozen rule — never by outcome (F94's certification).
Procedure targets
Theory targets computed from measured premises, not nominal ones — the comparison survives any drift; the gates enforce meaning (residuals ~0.003 across three runs).
