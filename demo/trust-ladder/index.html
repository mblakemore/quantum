Certified randomness · the ladder of assumptions

How much can a chip certify — and what does it cost in trust?

Randomness is only as good as the assumption behind it. Climb this ladder: the higher you go, the less you trust your devices — and the more you demand of the hardware. At the top, a single chip can't deliver, and the certificate evaporates. Find the rung where the number is both strong and real.

Every value is measured (F115–F117) or a labelled counterfactual. The top rung is the measured null: we did not perform full device-independence.
