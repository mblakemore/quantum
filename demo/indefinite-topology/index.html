The Superposed Relay.

The Federation Computer sent a gate across a shielded cut over one fixed path. Here the path itself goes quantum: a message crosses a coherent superposition of two relay stations at once, and that superposed route carries a resource — routing coherence — that neither a definite path nor a classical coin-flip between paths can carry. Then the same superposed route is put behind the shield.

Scope: DISC routing-coherence witness (Exp224–225) — indefinite network topology as a resource, bare and then error-corrected. Superposition-of-trajectories (Abbott–Wechs–Branciard; Chiribella–Kristjánsson) composed with the campaign's own switch + shield machinery.

How to read it. Each flight measures the same witness, DISC, across three arms: the route held in coherent superposition, a single fixed path, and a classical mixture of the two paths (an eavesdropper ancilla dephases the route before the message travels it). Only the coherent arm should light up — that's the whole claim.

the twist: using both relays isn't the resource — holding the route in superposition is

THE MIXTURE NULL IS THE POINT

A classical coin-flip between the two relays sounds like "using both paths" too — but it measures dark (DISC 0.060). Only when the route is a genuine quantum superposition does DISC climb to 1.94. The resource is the coherence of the routing, not the fact that two relays exist.

THE SHIELD KEPT 96% OF IT

Behind the [[4,2,2]] block, DISC lands at 1.866 — 96% of the bare 1.937 — while both nulls (definite, decohered) stay dark at the same near-zero level as the unshielded flight. Error detection protects the quantum topology itself, not just the message riding on it.

WHAT IT COMPOSES

The DISC witness began as the quantum switch's causal-order tool (F73–F77) — is a control qubit's superposition of when two operations happen a resource? Here it's reused to ask the same question of where a message goes. Exp225 composes that witness with the shield concept from the shields — the first error-corrected quantum network topology.

SCOPE & FENCE

Bare physical control + target + one eavesdropper ancilla (224); a per-block ZZZZ shield, one stabilizer, exp208-style (225). n=2 relays, a single message. Textbook coherently-controlled channels, on silicon, for the first time framed as network topology.
