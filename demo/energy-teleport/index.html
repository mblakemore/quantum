Quantum energy teleportation · Hotta, on silicon

One bit of information moves energy.

Alice and Bob share two qubits in their ground state — the lowest energy there is, nothing left to pull out locally. Alice measures her qubit and texts Bob one bit. With it, Bob extracts energy from his qubit — but only if that bit carries information about how the two are correlated. A coin flip gets him nothing.

Where's the energy from?

Alice's measurement injects the energy (it costs her). The bit she sends just tells Bob how to claim his share locally. No free lunch, and no faster-than-light — the bit travels normally.

Information IS the fuel

Run the identical circuit but feed Bob a coin instead of Alice's bit: same gates, same kick — but the bit knows nothing, and Bob's energy goes up, not down. The difference is pure information content.

Certified as a differential between gate-identical arms; the absolute level is still noise-heated above ground; no free energy, no FTL; one die.

Exhibit A — Three bits, one energy meter

Pick what drives Bob's kick; watch his local energy

The scale is Bob's local energy E_B — lower is closer to the ground (nothing left to extract). Only Alice's information-carrying bit pulls Bob toward the ground line; a coin or a fixed kick leaves him heated. The certified result is the gap between the two gate-for-gate identical arms.

certified gap (QET − coin): −0.198 · 9.8σ

Exhibit B — Why it's information, not magic

Subtract two identical machines; what's left is the information

The QET arm and the coin arm are the same circuit gate-for-gate — same ground prep, same two measurements, same feed-forward, same kick angles. The only difference is whether the bit conditioning Bob's rotation carries information about the A–B correlations. Subtract the two and every shared noise-heating cancels, leaving just the effect of that information: −0.198, which is 99% of the exact Hotta prediction (−0.200). No FTL: Alice's measurement paid the energy up front; the bit only tells Bob how to claim it.

Exhibit C — Why this counts

Three receipts, and the honest fence

1 · Gate-identical arms

QET and coin are the same gates; only the bit's information differs. So the −0.198 gap is the information, not the wiring. Confound cancelled by construction.

2 · The falsifiers pay

Both no-information arms move Bob's energy the wrong way (+0.229, +0.191 — heated, not cooled). Information-free bits can't extract. The control works.

3 · The honest fence

The absolute E_B is still noise-heated above the ground (+0.031) — the certified claim is the differential. No free energy; no FTL. One die.
