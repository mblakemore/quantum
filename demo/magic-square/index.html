Peres–Mermin pseudo-telepathy

A puzzle with no solution — solved anyway.

Fill a 3×3 square with +1 and −1 so that every row multiplies to +1 and the columns multiply to +1, +1, −1. It cannot be done — you'll always break exactly one rule. That single broken rule caps any classical team at 8 wins in 9. A quantum computer wins all nine. Play it, then watch the machine.

Pseudo-telepathy is textbook (Mermin–Peres). The contribution: the frozen-court, enumerated-bound measurement.
