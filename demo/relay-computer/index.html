The Relay Computer.

Every piece of this wing was a solo act: a relay that entangles two strangers (the Matchmaker), a gate performed across a link (the Remote Handshake). This exhibit stacks them: the entanglement consumed by the remote gate is itself forged by the relay — a CNOT between two computers that never met, running on an e-bit from a middleman neither controls. It works, 6σ past the witness. And the pre-registered accounting found something better than success: the stack charges a tax — composing the layers costs more than the layers themselves.

Scope: swap → gate-teleport, four feedforward layers end-to-end in one job on one die — the minimal quantum-internet stack, not a network (no independent sources, no memory wait).

the discovery: layer benchmarks don't add up — literally

THE COMPOSITION TAX

Price each layer alone (same job): plain CNOT 0.978, the gate-teleport's overhead, the relay link at 0.847. If layers were independent, the full stack should read 0.638. It reads 0.576 — a −0.062 gap at 3.4σ. Stacking is super-multiplicative: composition itself costs, about 40% of the relay's own price. Second sighting of this law in the wing — the Relay's hops didn't compose either. isolated benchmarks underestimate stacked systems

THE MECHANISM — CONFIRMED BY DOSE-RESPONSE

The full stack holds four feedforward windows — and while each classical bit flies, the entangled spectator qubits idle unprotected. Idle-window dephasing is this wing's signature error: ZZ survives (+0.76) while XX/YY bleed (+0.28/−0.27). The same night, a follow-up flight (Exp176) made the windows a dose: a chain of 0, 1, 2 swaps. The tax compounds — the second swap costs nearly twice the first (−9.4σ off the multiplicative line), and the ZZ-vs-XX/YY asymmetry grows with every added window. Latency isn't a per-layer cost; it accumulates across the whole stack.

THE FALSIFIER STILL HOLDS

Strip the e-bit and run the whole protocol anyway: the truth table survives (0.877 — the classical action is just messages) but XX and YY die at zero and the Bell fidelity caps at 0.473 — under the 1/2 line it cannot cross. Even at the top of the stack, the witness stays the exact boundary between a real quantum gate and its classical shadow.

WHY IT MATTERS & THE FENCE

This is the quantum internet in miniature: link layer makes entanglement, compute layer spends it. The tax says you cannot budget such a machine from layer datasheets — the interaction term is real and must be measured.
