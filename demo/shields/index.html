THE TOLL IS THE TREASURE

What it buys, three times over: logical Z-error at HALF the bare rate (0.0010 vs 0.0021); logical entanglement above bare (1.970 vs 1.902); logical teleportation above bare (0.9933 vs 0.9237). THE FAILURE THAT DREW THE MAP

Stage (ii)'s first flight failed ALL THREE rungs — flown at a dose where distance-2 detection mathematically loses, with an unfair echo and mis-nulled bars (three owned design flaws, each now a checklist rule). The redesign then measured the full pays-curve: the crossover sits at ≈1.2–1.5 μs. The hollow points on the stage-(ii) plot ARE the failed flight — the map exists because of it.
