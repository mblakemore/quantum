Kit reports resolve against honest theory odds per case (Rookie: separate interviews; Casefile: the entangled comparison — the strongest kit we know, deck average 75%, and it is always wrong when a do-nothing suspect is involved; you'll meet that as unexplained losses, and the reveal will show you why). That is a theorem (Araújo et al. 2015; bound re-derived and machine-verified in this repo).
