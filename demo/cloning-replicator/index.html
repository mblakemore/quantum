A limit on quantum itself · nothing beats it

Copy a qubit — the universe caps you at 5/6, and every cheat gets caught.

The no-cloning theorem says you cannot perfectly copy an unknown quantum state. You can make imperfect copies — and there's a proven best: the optimal universal cloner makes two copies each at fidelity exactly 5/6 ≈ 83.3%, the same for every possible input. You cannot do better than 5/6 for all states at once. This exhibit certifies that ceiling on hardware — and runs a cheat alongside to prove the ceiling has teeth.

The trap, in one line

The only way to beat 5/6 somewhere is to get caught somewhere else. A copier tuned for one measurement direction can score 99% there — but it necessarily craters to a coin-flip (50%) on the two conjugate directions. Universality is the certificate no cheat can forge.

Certifies a quantum limit (the completing move to the no-go games), not a speedup. 1→2 symmetric universal cloning · device-characterized · single run.

Exhibit B — The cheat-detector

Flatness is the measurement — the null weaponized

The cheat arm was pre-registered to fail, and its failure mode is the detector. An honest universal cloner is the same in every basis (tiny spread); any basis-reading cheat is loud (huge spread). So you don't need to know a copier's secret — just measure how much it varies across bases.

The optimal cloner's basis spread is 0.022; the cheat's is 0.49 — a ~24× separation. Above the detector threshold, a copier is provably reading a secret basis. Universality (flatness) is the signature no cheat can forge, because beating 5/6 in one basis mathematically forces a loss in its conjugate.

Exhibit C — What's real here

The receipts

The ceiling is textbook and the cheat is a control designed to fail — the contribution is the frozen-court, executed-cheat-arm, universality-flatness certification on silicon.

1 · The ceiling is proven, not cited

5/6 is the optimal universal-cloning fidelity (Bužek–Hillery 1996; Gisin–Massar). The cloner circuit was numerically optimized and verified in-artifact — noiseless mean 0.83332, cross-state variance 2.2×10⁻⁸. Not memorized.

2 · Saturated, never exceeded

Optimal measured Z 0.8265 · Y 0.8121 · X 0.8047 — flat (spread 0.022), a hair below the line; the ~0.019 gap is pure 11-CZ noise. Exceeding 5/6 anywhere would be the tell of secret basis-reading. It never does.

3 · The teeth, on the same chip

The cheat wins Z 0.9911 but pays X 0.4995 · Y 0.5054 — min-over-bases 0.4995. All five gates PASS (sentinels 0.991 / 0.973). The way to win is the way to get caught.
