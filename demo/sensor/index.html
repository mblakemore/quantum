The Sensor.

An instrument is its error bar. We sealed a secret field strength behind a hash, sent two sensors to read it blind — five ordinary probes vs five entangled ones — and made each publish its answer with its uncertainty before the seal opened. The entangled sensor was five times sharper… and on the first flight its error bar lied by 8σ. The protocol caught it, the fix flew the same hour, and the certified sensor now reads the hidden field to 0.2σ — still 3.7× beyond any classical strategy at the same budget.

Scope: blind sealed-phase estimation on 5 qubits (GHZ vs product probes, matched budget) — a certification protocol demo, not a field-deployed magnetometer; dynamic range 2π/5.

Open the seal

two flights, same protocol · ibm_fez · truth hidden behind sha256 until decode

How to read it. Each flight: a secret θ* is drawn and its hash committed publicly before launch. The product sensor (5 independent probes, classical strategy) and the GHZ sensor (5 qubits entangled — the field's kick is amplified 5×) each publish an estimate and a 95% confidence interval computed from their own data. Then the seal opens and the ruler below shows where truth actually was. A sensor passes only if its interval contains the truth — precision means nothing if the bar doesn't cover.

WHY THE FIRST ERROR BAR LIED

The GHZ fringe runs 5× steeper — which also amplifies any systematic phase 5×. The first flight calibrated with a single θ=0 arm, and cosine is an even function: one calibration point cannot distinguish reduced contrast from a phase offset. A ~0.03 rad systematic rode in unmodeled; the statistical bar didn't know it existed. More information per shot ≠ more truth per shot — unless the calibration model can see every parameter the slope amplifies.

THE FIX, PROVEN BEFORE IT FLEW

Two calibration arms (applied 0 and a known 0.35 rad) give three fringe points for three unknowns — contrast, offset, field. The pre-flight truth-gate injects flight 1's disease into simulation: the pipeline measures the planted −0.030 as −0.0325 and still covers truth. On hardware it then measured the real offset at −0.0345 — the same systematic, quasi-static across both jobs — and corrected it blind.

WHAT SURVIVES ALL GATES

Entanglement advantage: variance ratio 0.271 at matched budget (ideal 0.20 — Heisenberg; classical floor 1.0), coverage HIT in both arms, truth revealed only after the estimates were frozen.
