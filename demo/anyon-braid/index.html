The Anyon Loop.

In two dimensions, particles are allowed a third option — neither boson nor fermion. On a 7-qubit patch of topological matter, carrying one kind of quasiparticle in a closed loop around the other writes a phase of exactly −1 into an interferometer. Six experimental arms show the minus sign obeys only topology: it counts the charge enclosed, mod 2, and cares nothing for the loop's shape, or for charge sitting just outside. Each arm read at ~50σ on real silicon.

Scope: abelian Z₂ mutual statistics (toric-code e and m), read by single-shot Wilson-loop interferometry — not non-abelian braiding, not adiabatic transport, not fault tolerance.

Start here — flatland particles, in plain words

no equations · why a third kind of particle is allowed, and why anyone cares

Every particle you have ever met is one of two kinds. Swap two identical bosons and the universe shrugs. Swap two identical fermions and the universe's bookkeeping flips a minus sign. In our 3D world those are the only options, because of a quiet geometric fact: any trip that carries one particle around another can be lifted into the third dimension and shrunk away to nothing. The trip leaves no trace.

Squash the world flat and that escape route disappears. In two dimensions, a particle carried around another leaves a permanent tangle in their shared history — plot position against time and the two world-lines are braided like rope. No smooth motion can undo a braid without cutting it. So flatland is allowed a third kind of particle: one that remembers being circled. Physicists named them anyons, because the memory can be "any" phase.

That is what the panel below shows, with real hardware data: we built a small patch of digital flatland (seven qubits of "toric-code" matter, plus one interferometer qubit), placed two kinds of quasiparticles on it, carried one around the other, and asked what the trip left behind. The answer is exactly a minus sign — and the six arms show the sign obeys braid-logic, not geometry: it appears only when the loop truly encloses the other particle, it does not care about the loop's shape, and circling two of them cancels out (minus times minus).

Why this is a building block. The minus sign is stored in the braid — in the whole history — not in any qubit you could point to. The receipts panel shows that directly: every individual qubit on the patch reads as a fair coin, yet the braid answer is certain at 50σ. Information that no local poke can read is information that no local noise can easily corrupt — and that is the founding idea of topological quantum computing: protect quantum information by hiding it in topology.

THIS EXHIBIT — ABELIAN ANYONS (the first rung)

The braid writes a phase: a minus sign. It proves braid-memory is real and behaves topologically — but a phase alone doesn't compute. Toric-code e and m quasiparticles, mutual statistics π, every falsifier on the table. this page: Exp157, ibm_fez, 6 arms at ~50σ

THE NEXT RUNG — NON-ABELIAN ANYONS (where braids compute)

For a rarer kind of anyon, the braid changes the state itself — swapping two of them moves the system to a genuinely different configuration, so a sequence of swaps IS a computation. Google Quantum AI demonstrated this in 2023 (D3V graph-vertex anyons on a superconducting chip: eight anyons encoding three logical qubits, entangled into a GHZ state by braiding alone). Same hardware family as this exhibit; the rung this one is built to hold up. Google Quantum AI, Nature 618, 264 (2023)

The court

why the minus sign is topological — and exactly what it is not

SIX ARMS CLOSE EACH OTHER'S LOOPHOLES

−1 appears only for odd enclosed charge: empty loop +1, one m −1, the same m outside the loop +1, a deformed loop around the same m −1, an enclosed pair +1, a straddling pair −1. A classical parity artifact would have to match the outside, deformation, and fusion patterns simultaneously — it cannot. all six pre-registered, all >50σ

THE STATE IS THE OTHER HALF OF THE PROOF

Loop values are only topology if the state carries loop order: plaquettes +0.86/+0.82 and stars 0.87–0.95 certain, while all seven single edges read ⟨Z⟩ = 0.00–0.06 — a loop-gas superposition, not a bit-string. receipts measured on the same prepared state
