A discovery from the Heron 2026 campaign

The Tricorder.

Point the instrument at a hydrogen molecule and read off its physics. A quantum chip scans H2 across ten bond lengths and recovers the dissociation curve, the bond length — within 0.04 Å of where a real hydrogen molecule sits — and the electron correlation that mean-field theory provably misses, detected at 31σ. The chemistry it verifies against was derived from scratch: Gaussian integrals in plain numpy, no chemistry package anywhere in the stack.

Scope: hardware energy readout of a first-principles 2-qubit molecular Hamiltonian (minimal STO-3G basis) — verified against the model's exact diagonalization, not spectroscopy; no mitigation.

The scan

energy vs bond length · 10 hardware points · ibm_fez · 4096 shots × 3 bases each

How it works. For each bond length R, the exact correlated ground state of the molecule's 2-qubit Hamiltonian is prepared with a single CX, and the energy is measured in three bases (⟨Z₀⟩, ⟨Z₁⟩, ⟨Z₀Z₁⟩, ⟨X₀X₁⟩, ⟨Y₀Y₁⟩ recombined with the self-derived coefficients). The variational theorem makes this self-verifying: no physical state can have energy below the true ground energy, so hardware noise can only push the reading up. A point below the exact curve would mean the instrument is lying. None is.

What the instrument recovered

three falsifiers armed pre-flight · all passed · raw hardware, no error mitigation

The readings carry a systematic offset — on average +65 mHa above exact, ~40× chemical accuracy, largest where the Hamiltonian's coefficients are strongest. But the offset is nearly constant, so differences survive: the binding energy (bottom of the well vs the dissociated atoms) comes out 191 mHa vs the model's 201 — within 5%. A noisy instrument tells the truth in derived quantities: curve shape, bond length, binding, and the correlation gap.

The court

why this reading is believable — and exactly what it is not

THE INSTRUMENT CANNOT FLATTER ITSELF

⟨ψ|H|ψ⟩ ≥ Eground for any state — so every error mechanism (gate noise, readout, decoherence) raises the reading. All 10 points sit above the exact curve. A single point significantly below would have voided the run as an instrument fault. 0/10 violations at 3σ

THE MATCHED CONTROL MUST FAIL

The Hartree-Fock arm runs the identical circuit with θ=0 — same gates, no correlation. It must sit above the correlated arm by the correlation energy, which grows 11× toward dissociation as mean-field theory fails to break the bond. The hardware sees exactly that divergence. 202 mHa at 2.5 Å, 31σ

CHEMISTRY FROM SCRATCH

No chemistry packages: STO-3G integrals from Szabo-Ostlund closed forms in numpy (Boys function, two-electron integrals), 2×2 full CI, self-derived Pauli coefficients. Cross-checked against the literature before flight: RHF −1.1167, FCI −1.1373 Ha at R=1.4 bohr — to four decimals. truth-gates A/B/C all passed first run

THE FENCE

Minimal-basis model of H₂ on 2 qubits, verified against its own exact diagonalization — not against experimental spectroscopy (STO-3G overbinds real H₂). Absolute accuracy is 65 mHa raw; chemical accuracy would need mitigation and a bigger basis. A hardware energy readout with intrinsic falsifiers, not a beyond-classical chemistry calculation.
