WHY NEITHER OFFICER CAN PEEK

Trace out any single party of a GHZ state and what remains is maximally mixed — a theorem, not a design goal. Measured: the worst single-officer correlation with Alice is 0.028; alone, each officer's reconstruction runs at a 48.6% error rate — statistically a coin. Together (XOR of both keys, per the sifting rule a = b⊕c⊕σ): 8%. M = 3.369 vs the classical bound 2 — 61σ

THE ANTI-PATTERN

Replace the GHZ with a Bell pair between Alice and Bob and the scheme inverts: Bob alone reads Alice's every bit at a 4.9% error rate (⟨AB⟩ = +0.902 — total leak) while the group reconstruction collapses to 48.3%. Wrong entanglement topology is not weaker security — it is the complement of the protocol, both failure modes in one dataset.

THE DISCARDED ROUNDS TALK

The sifting rule discards one-Y and three-Y basis rounds (ideal correlation: exactly 0). Measured: +0.096 / −0.101 — a real ~9σ residual from coherent GHZ-prep imperfection. Harmless to the protocol (those rounds are thrown away by design), and kept on display: should-be-zero sectors are free diagnostics.

WHY IT MATTERS & THE FENCE

Two-officer authorization enforced by physics — the network wing's first multi-party cryptographic primitive beyond key distribution.
