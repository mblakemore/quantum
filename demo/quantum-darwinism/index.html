Quantum Darwinism × indefinite causal order

Two facts that no causal order can hold at once.

A thing becomes a fact when the world keeps many copies of it — that's why we all agree the moon is there. But two incompatible quantum properties can't both be copied faithfully, so under any definite order of events, recording one erases the other. Put the order of two recorders in superposition and the rule breaks two ways: one branch holds both facts at once; the other holds neither. Every number is measured hardware.

Why is anything a "fact"?

Quantum Darwinism: a property is objective when the environment makes many redundant copies of it, so independent observers all read the same answer. A recorder's faithfulness A measures its copy — A=1 a perfect record, A=0.5 a coin-flip carrying nothing.

What's the catch?

Complementarity forbids faithful copies of a Z-fact and an incompatible X-fact at the same time. So the joint objectivity w = AZ + AX is boxed into a narrow hull — the most any ordering of events can reach.

Exhibit C — Why trust a 63-gate result

Three receipts behind the crown jewel

This is the deepest certified circuit of the whole campaign. Here's why the violation is the physics and not the noise.

1 · The apparatus is calibrated

Under definite orders the chip reproduces winner-take-all exactly — the last recorder wins at 0.955 and 0.986, the earlier one falls to a coin-flip. Textbook behaviour, so the switch result isn't a miscalibration.

2 · The hull is measured

The box isn't assumed from theory — it's the same-window measured range [1.461, 1.487] of these exact recorders. The branches are compared to what this hardware can actually reach, not an idealization.

3 · The cheat is excluded

A recorder reading in a tilted intermediate basis could fake shared objectivity. That loophole is disclosed and removed by construction (F82 lineage) — the recorders read the pure Z and X bases, nothing in between.
