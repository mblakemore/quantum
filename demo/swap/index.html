The Matchmaker.

Two qubits, A and C, sit at opposite ends of the chip. No gate ever connects them; they share no history. Between them, two Bell pairs and one measurement — and when the middle qubits are measured together and two classical bits are sent to C, A and C become entangled with each other. Certified: overlap with a perfect Bell pair F = 0.836, where no unentangled pair of qubits can exceed 1/2 — a 40σ violation of the separable bound. This is entanglement swapping, the primitive every quantum repeater is built from.

Scope: the swapping primitive on one die (zero storage, adjacent routing) — not a repeater: no quantum memory, no purification, no independent sources.

The memory — how long the match keeps

swap, then hold τ before the witness reads · Exp163/164 · same pinned qubits · jobs d9dtf1aneu4c739nnq20, d9dtmbcinv1c73aphifg

How to read it. A repeater must hold entanglement, not just make it. Each curve: swap A–C, store for τ, then measure the Bell fidelity. Above the dashed 1/2 line the match is still provably entangled; where a curve crosses is its certified hold time. The echo arms fire a single X on both qubits mid-storage — |Φ+⟩ is a +1 eigenstate of X⊗X, so the pulse leaves the state untouched while refocusing each qubit's slow detuning drift.

The echo doubles the memory: 16 → 36 μs certified hold time (2.3×). A second echo (CPMG-2, 38 μs) adds nothing — one π-pulse captures all the refocusable noise, pinning the dephasing as quasi-static. Two notes: the swapped pair ages exactly like a gate-made pair (pedigree ratio 0.76 — decay is local physics, not pedigree), and at very long τ the plain arm floats higher (~0.37, its classical ZZ correlation survives dephasing) while echo pulse errors accumulate — the echo wins where the memory is usable, not asymptotically. The distillery — two faded pairs, one brighter pair

entanglement purification (DEJMPS) · Exp165 · ibm_fez · 8000 shots · job d9dtr24inv1c73aphomg

How to read it. When storage has faded two Bell pairs, a repeater can trade them: local rotations, a CNOT between the pairs on each side, then measure one pair and keep only the shots where its two bits agree. Half the data is spent — and the surviving pair comes back brighter. The bars show the witness fidelity: the faded input, the kept pair after distillation, and the discard pile (the rejected shots) — which must come out worse than the input, or the protocol isn't sorting anything.

Gain +0.292 at 20σ, success probability 0.50 — and the sort is visible in the same shots: kept 0.688, discard −0.171. The remarkable part, fenced: this run's input had faded to 0.396, below the 1/2 witness — not even certifiably entangled — yet the distilled output is certified. No bound is violated: the textbook "purification needs F > 1/2" threshold is a statement about unstructured (Werner) noise, and this storage noise is structured coherent dephasing — diagnosed by the memory panel above, and exactly the error class this protocol filters (proven in simulation before flight). It would not work on 0.396 of depolarized noise. Distillation exploits structure; that's the physics, not magic.

The court

why the certificate is airtight — and where this sits in the network wing

THE BOUND IS A THEOREM

For any two qubits in a separable (unentangled) state, the overlap with a maximally entangled state is at most 1/2 — no noise model, no loophole. 0.836 clears it by 40σ. The certificate needs no trust in the preparation: it is read off A and C alone. F = (1+ZZ+XX−YY)/4 · pre-registered

BOTH INGREDIENTS PROVEN LOAD-BEARING

Skip the classical feedforward: the four Bell outcomes average to the maximally mixed state — F drops to 0.258. Skip the Bell measurement entirely: A and C are never entangled — 0.251. Both falsifiers sit exactly at the 0.25 floor; only the full protocol produces the match.

THE PRICE, MEASURED

A directly-prepared Bell pair in the same job reads 0.979, so the swap costs 0.143 — and the loss is basis-structured (ZZ 0.86 vs XX/YY 0.74): qubit A idles, entangled and unechoed, through the measure-and- correct window. The same spectator-idle fingerprint the relay found — the day's error taxonomy, consistent across experiments. within-job baseline · C4199 rule

The network wing's three primitives, all flown in one day, each past its own classical bound with falsifiers intact: the hop (0.913 vs 2/3), the chain (0.784 end-to-end, 45σ), and the swap (0.836 vs 1/2, 40σ). Teleport a state, relay it, entangle strangers. Exp154 · Exp160 · Exp162
