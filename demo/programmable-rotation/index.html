Universality · the gate set, dialed

Dial a universal protected qubit.

A protected qubit is only useful if you can compute anything with it — not a fixed few moves. Pick a program and watch the qubit rotate to point exactly where you told it, anywhere around the dial. Then turn off the special ingredient — the "magic" — and watch the reach collapse. That reachable "anywhere" is what universal means.

Cheap moves vs magic

Clifford gates are the cheap calculator moves — fast, but a classical computer can simulate them, and they can't reach the diagonal points. The T ("magic") gate is the special ingredient that unlocks every rotation.

Why "dialing" = universal

A famous no-go (Eastin–Knill) says you can't get the full toolkit for free on a protected qubit. The fix: inject a magic state and steer it with cheap programs. Reaching the off-axis targets is the proof it's universal.

Error-detected; a single injected T on a few qubits is classically simulable — this is universality's mechanism, not a supremacy claim.

Exhibit A — The dial

Pick a program; watch the protected qubit rotate to its target

The circle is the Bloch equator (X̄ axis →, Ȳ axis ↑). Each program steers the injected magic state to one of four non-stabilizer targets — points no cheap-Clifford state can occupy. The faint ring is the ideal target; the solid dot is the measured hardware value. Then flip the resource to a plain Clifford and watch the reach vanish onto the axis.

Exhibit B — Why the dial means "universal"

Cheap calculator, or a computer that can run anything

A quantum computer is universal when it can approximate any operation. Cliffords alone can't — they're stuck on the grid (and a laptop can simulate them). Add the injected T and you reach the whole dial.

◇ Clifford only — the cheap calculator

Fast, protected, useful for setup — but classically simulable (Gottesman–Knill) and can't leave the axes. On the dial: stuck on the Ȳ axis.

✦ Clifford + T — universal

Add one injected magic gate and the protected qubit can be dialed anywhere — the four measured diagonal targets are points no Clifford can reach. Exp244 closed the gate set; Exp246 dialed it.

Three receipts, and the fence
