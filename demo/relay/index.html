The Relay.

A network is not a link — it is links composed. Here a qubit's state crosses two teleportation hops in sequence: destroyed at the source, rebuilt at a midpoint, destroyed again, rebuilt at the far end — two Bell pairs, two live feedforward corrections. End-to-end fidelity 0.784, past the 2/3 classical relay bound at 45σ. And a surprise in the accounting: the hops don't compose — the second window costs more than the first, and the deficit has a fingerprint.

Scope: a relay primitive (sequential teleportation on one die, zero storage) — not a repeater: no entanglement swapping, no purification, no quantum memory.

The court

THE CHANNEL IS QUANTUM END-TO-END

The 2/3 bound applies to the whole chain — a classical middleman anywhere caps it. 0.784 clears it at 45σ, and hop 2's own falsifiers (Bell pair removed → superpositions to chance; corrections removed → everything to chance) prove the second hop's resources are load-bearing, independent of hop 1's (Exp154). within-job, pre-registered

THE DEFICIT HAS A FINGERPRINT

The −0.095 shortfall vs composition is not uniform: the chain loses ~0.20 on superpositions and only ~0.06 on Z states, while the same-job single hop is nearly uniform. The end receiver idles through two feedforward windows — consistent with idle-window dephasing scaling superlinearly, the Exp154/158 error class re-emerging in exactly the condition that doubles the window. Consistent-with, not proven.

THE MISS

Pre-registered band 0.855–0.92 assumed hops compose; they don't (0.784). The composition check was built into the decode precisely to expose this — the named risk materialized with its diagnostic attached. prediction ledger updated, calibration 82.3%

THE ARMED TEST FLEW — AND THE CONDITION MOVED

DD on the relay's receiver flew one hour later (Exp161): the 0.17 gap had already decayed to 0.099 — below the pre-registered threshold — and the small positive signal (z=2.4, ~8% recovery, controls clean) fails the practical gate, so it stays unclaimed. Three DD tests in one day, zero resolvable: the idle-gap condition moves on sub-hour timescales and echoes can't catch it. Chapter closed.
