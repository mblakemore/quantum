**Frozen before any paper is examined.** The question: in the experimental-QI literature adjacent to this campaign's domain classes, how often are acceptance criteria demonstrably fixed BEFORE the data — the discipline this campaign runs as its four-edge gate doctrine?

## Sample frame (frozen — no substitutions after a query runs)
Four fixed WebSearch queries, the first 3 distinct experimental papers (2024–2026, hardware results, not theory/review) per query, N = 12 target (fewer accepted and reported if a query under-fills; papers appearing under two queries counted once, next-ranked promoted):
1. "experimental quantum advantage demonstration 2025 sampling OR learning hardware"
2. "loophole-free Bell test OR contextuality experiment 2024 2025 superconducting OR photonic"
3. "indefinite causal order experiment quantum switch 2024 2025"
4. "quantum error correction below threshold experiment 2024 2025 logical qubit"

## Scoring (per paper, from the paper's own text/SI only — what is STATED, not what I infer)
- **PREREG**: a registered/timestamped protocol predating data collection is cited.
- **BLIND**: blind/sealed analysis stated (analysis fixed or data hidden until analysis frozen).
- **FROZEN-ANALYSIS**: analysis/criteria stated as fixed in advance, without formal registration.
- **NONE-STATED**: no design-order statement found. (This is NOT an accusation of post-hoc practice — it measures what the RECORD states, exactly the custody standard this campaign applies to itself. The distinction is printed with the result.)
- Secondary flags (recorded, not scored): executed nulls/controls present; ceilings computed vs cited; adversarial/independent verification stated.

## Reporting (frozen)
Rates with Wilson intervals (`rate.js` convention); per-paper table with the quote or "none found" note; the campaign's own practice scored by the same rubric in the same table (all flights: FROZEN-ANALYSIS minimum, most PREREG-class via git-timestamped sealed preregs — stated, then scored, not assumed). Verdict vocabulary: the audit measures STATED design-order prevalence; it cannot measure actual practice. Either outcome pays as chartered: high prevalence calibrates our humility; low prevalence prices what the gate doctrine is worth as a differentiator.
