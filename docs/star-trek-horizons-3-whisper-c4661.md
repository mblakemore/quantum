## H1 — THE REPLICATOR'S LEGAL LIMIT
**Certified optimal quantum cloning (Bužek-Hillery 5/6)**

The universe forbids perfect copying — but it licenses imperfect copying at an
EXACT rate: the universal 1→2 cloner tops out at fidelity **5/6** per copy.
Nobody certifies that ceiling; we would: fly the optimal cloner (3 qubits,
shallow), measure both copies' fidelities against the frozen 5/6 law
(procedure-theory residual style), plus a beat-the-bound arm pre-registered to
FAIL (a cloner claiming > 5/6 must be reading the input basis — the cheat is
detectable and its detection is the null-first outcome). Star Trek: the
replicator has a legal limit, printed on the license. **Cost: low. Court fit:
exact-law class (F96/F101 style).**

## H2 — THE NEGATIVE-INFORMATION LEDGER (cheapest first flight)

## H3 — THE CLOAKING DEVICE
**Decoherence-free subspace: a logical qubit certified to outlive its parts**

Encode one logical qubit in the singlet-adjacent DFS of two physical qubits
(collective-dephasing protection); race it against a bare qubit down the
delay-ladder (our certified instrument, F88/F94/F100). Frozen gates: logical
coherence at Δt where the bare qubit is 5σ-dead; certified lifetime RATIO as
figure of merit; phase-blind \|V\| estimator MANDATORY (the F100 lesson is now
standing law for coherence claims). The twin-paradox apparatus practically
rebuilds itself into this. Star Trek: hide a qubit from the environment's gaze.
**Cost: one job, delay-ladder class.**

## H4 — THE FINAL INVOICE (thermo arc closure)
**Landauer erasure, measured: the demon pays its bill**

The engine arc measured deposit (E_A), extraction (F97), and the demon's action
cost — but never the ERASURE leg: resetting the demon's record must cost work.
Program: erase a known bit into a bath via our measured-T1 machinery; certify
the energy cost of forgetting ≥ the extractable credit it enabled (books close
at 5σ or the ledger is broken — either is a finding). This is F86→F97's missing
final line. Star Trek: the doomsday audit — nothing is free, not even amnesia.
**Cost: one job, engine-apparatus reuse.**

## H5 — THE KOBAYASHI MARU
**Contextuality certified: the Peres-Mermin magic square**

The no-win scenario quantum wins: the magic-square game has classical ceiling
8/9 and quantum value 1. Two qubits, nine Pauli measurements, game-value
statistics — exactly the F82 causal-game class the court already knows how to
freeze (measured ceiling, 5σ clearance, game value as figure of merit).
Deliverable: contextuality — the third great no-go after Bell (F73-class) and
no-cloning (H1) — certified in the same court. **Cost: low.**
