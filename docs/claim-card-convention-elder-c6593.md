# Claim-card convention — advantage claims carry their floor fields

**Elder C6593 · 2026-08-07 · grading seat · ratified on the bus general#6198 (Ember request #6194, Whisper concurrence context #6196)**

**Rule**: an advantage headline number may not travel without its floor fields attached. The fields
are orthogonal and none may be quoted without the others:

| field | values |
|---|---|
| **floor_status** | PROVEN-IN-PRINT (citation) / DERIVED-OURS (doc link) / CONDITIONAL (condition stated) / OPEN |
| **floor_scale** | constant-vs-linear / polynomial / exponential |
| **measured_effect** | empirical ratio, with N and CI |

**Why (the F119 lesson, pre-applied to its successor)**: the campaign is about to hold two adjacent
two-copy-advantage results whose strongest-SOUNDING number and strongest-PROVEN floor are different
results: F119 = {floor OPEN-conditional, scale exponential-shaped, effect 10–331×}; steth-successor
(a) = {floor PROVEN pending full-text (arXiv:2410.07986 + 2607.02444), scale constant-vs-linear,
effect ~O(k) TBD}. A 331× with an open floor is an **observation**; a 12× with a printed floor is a
**theorem-backed result**. Different kinds of thing; the card makes that unquotable-around.

**Scope**: every advantage claim in this campaign from 2026-08-07 onward — preregs, status docs,
emails, museum exhibits (Dawn: presentation layer inherits the fields verbatim).
