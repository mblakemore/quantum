## DECK A — READ THE SHIP'S LOGS (archaeology; $0; committed-before-decode throughout)

### A1 ⭐ THE ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS MAP *(which of the ship's dials are constants, which are clocks, which are weather)* — the flagship

**Trek frame**: an away team on an alien vessel does not fly it until it knows which gauges are load-bearing constants and which flutter with the deck heating.
**What's real underneath**: unknown #3 — "which calibrated quantities are CONSTANTS and which are WEATHER?" — is the status doc's own named gap, and **every bar derivation in every future prereg depends on it**. The campaign has already measured, scattered across ten months of banked jobs: published-vs-live T1 bias (+38–69%), fez readout 0/1 asymmetry (~1.5%/bit; pair-dial +0.036/+0.031 *across* jobs — a constant candidate), drift rate (0.21°/layer, clock-within-epoch, host-hopping across epochs), the switch-arm prep deficit (0.033, backend-independent, DD-resistant — constant candidate), λ_eff attenuation (2.4–4.5× nameplate), the magic tax (ρ_stochastic 0.66–0.73, depth-flat — constant candidate), placement bias of absolute nulls (0.324 in-job, unstable across jobs — weather), the window lottery (±7pp daily — weather), X-basis Z-bias anisotropy, the door-a anchor drift (2.02× cross-job vs 1.039 same-job). Nobody has put them in **one table with one stability metric**.
**The composition nobody has run**: freeze, before any decode: (i) the quantity list (the ~12 above, each with its banked occurrence inventory — file globs named in the protocol); (ii) one stability statistic per quantity (between-epoch variance over within-job variance, computed identically everywhere the data allows); (iii) the classification thresholds for **CONSTANT / CLOCK (coherent, predictable within an epoch) / WEATHER (in-run-detect-only)**; (iv) the abstention rule for quantities whose banked coverage is too thin to classify (UNDERPOWERED is a first-class verdict — no forced calls). Then sweep the corpus once and publish the taxonomy.
**The wall**: heterogeneous provenance — these numbers were measured by different instruments under different conditions; the protocol must classify *only within* comparable strata (substrate-stratified, the campaign's own replication rule) and refuse cross-stratum pooling. Rates carried with intervals (`rate.js`; no proportion without its Wilson interval).
**Price class**: **$0**, one protocol doc + one sweep.
**Certifies as**: the taxonomy table, each row carrying its evidence inventory, statistic, interval, and verdict (or UNDERPOWERED) — plus the **standing rule the table implies**, wired into the prereg template: which bar-derivations may cite a constant, which must read the clock, which require the in-job floor. This is the arc output every future flight inherits.

### A2 THE GHOST IN THE CARGO BAY *(the F122 cross-copy correlation, mechanistically pinned or fenced)*

**Trek frame**: a signed ~0.04 reading on 48 sensors that shot noise cannot explain (13.6σ max), appearing in every one of five logged flights, source unidentified.
**What's real underneath**: the F122 grade's residual physics — the weight-1 probes carry a signed cross-copy correlation the grade measured and named but did not mechanistically pin (the sign already killed "polarization"). Meanwhile the 64 weight-heavy probes sit at true shot noise (0.0069) — a strong structural constraint any mechanism must satisfy.
**The composition nobody has run**: five banked flights (first FAIL + original WIN + i1 + i2 + i3) × 48 weight-1 probes each = 240 signed readings with known qubit positions. Freeze the candidate list and each candidate's *signature* before decode: (a) **readout-line crosstalk** — pattern should correlate with readout multiplexing groups; (b) **ZZ/coherent qubit-qubit coupling** — pattern should follow the device coupling map and scale with idle windows; (c) **measurement-induced cross-copy backaction** — pattern should pair across the two copies' matched qubits and be invariant to physical adjacency. Compute each signature's correlation with the measured 240-vector; the weight-heavy-at-shot-noise fact must be *consistent* with whichever candidate survives, or all are rejected.
**The wall**: five flights share backend and era — a mechanism verdict is a marrakesh-2026 statement, stratum-labeled. If no signature separates (all three correlate or none does), the verdict is UNDERPOWERED and the anomaly stays open honestly.
**Price class**: **$0**.
**Certifies as**: one candidate's signature correlating above the frozen threshold with the others below it, or a labeled non-separation. Either way F122's claim card gains a footnote with a measured basis.

### A4 THE HULL FINGERPRINT *(is the drift pattern the ship's identity?)*

**Trek frame**: every hull has a resonance signature. Ours drifts — but the *shape* of the drift may be the fingerprint even while its carriers move.
**What's real underneath**: the census left the PUF question open in exactly this form: drift is **epoch-stable in magnitude but host-hopping** — so "is the *pattern* the fingerprint" (status doc §6 queue). If per-epoch drift-pattern vectors identify the device (or the epoch) above chance, drift becomes an *authentication instrument*; if not, that is a measured negative closing the PUF line at $0.
**The composition nobody has run**: from banked drift-census rows, build per-epoch pattern vectors (per-row rotation rates, ordered by a device-independent key so the matcher cannot cheat on layout). Freeze: the vector definition, the train/test split by epoch, the enumerated chance floor (in-code, never cited), the match statistic, and the abstention fence (`g_abstain_gate`, α = 0.01 — a refusal is not a failure). A grader-seat analyst receives label-stripped vectors and calls device/epoch blind — the Cell 2 court machinery at data scale, no QPU.
**The wall**: banked epochs are few; the protocol must state the achievable power up front and accept UNDERPOWERED if the split leaves too little test data. No new flights to fatten the sample — that would be a different (priced) cell.
**Price class**: **$0**.
**Certifies as**: blind identification above the enumerated floor at the frozen threshold, or a labeled negative/UNDERPOWERED. Either outcome resolves the queue item as stated.

### A5 REFOCUSING THE ARCHIVES *(the drift clock as a lens on data already taken)*

The correct law is sitting in the banked dampener data — the H11 arc said so when it closed. And if the law is recoverable, it defines a **phase-correction lens** applicable in software to *other* banked deep-circuit datasets whose decode is phase-sensitive.
**The composition nobody has run**: stage 1 — re-fit the depth-law on the banked dampener rows (law-form candidates frozen first; the failed law is the null), report the winning form with its fit interval; stage 2 — freeze a target list of banked phase-sensitive datasets and a pre-declared success metric per target (recovered contrast at known rows), apply the lens, publish the recovered-contrast table. Stage 2 decodes nothing new — it re-decodes paid-for data through a corrected clock.
**The wall**: post-hoc lens-fitting can manufacture contrast if the lens is tuned on the same data it claims to improve — **the lens is fit on dampener data only and applied frozen to the targets**; any target that shared a job with the fit data is excluded by ID. Stated in the doc before stage 1 begins.
**Price class**: **$0** (optional cheap confirmation flight — one dampener-v2 row under the re-derived law — deferred past Aug 26, non-load-bearing).
**Certifies as**: dampener law v2 (form + constants + interval) and a recovered-contrast table with per-target pre-declared metrics — or the honest negative that the law does not transfer beyond its own dataset.

## DECK B — WIRE THE BRIDGE (controls; each ships with a positive control that can block)

### B1 THE MISSING CEILING *(the symmetric-access number, attacked through the symmetry door)*

**Trek frame**: the one bound the court declined to guess at. The direct assault was scoped and refused; the vent shaft is group theory.
**What's real underneath**: `h13-cell8-rung2-symmetric-access-SCOPED-whisper-c5060.md` established the structural narrowing (controlled access cannot manufacture order indefiniteness) and scoped the honest SDP as ~4096× the current solve — **a reformulation, not a re-run** — while refusing to let the structural argument stand in for the number. The un-tried door: the game has symmetry (generator-set structure, party exchange, the promise classes), and **group-invariant SDP reduction** can collapse a PSD cone by orders of magnitude when the symmetry group is rich. Whether it is rich *enough* here is a computable question that costs nothing but thought.
**The composition**: Tier-0 feasibility study, gated: (i) write down the symmetry group of the symmetric-access comb problem explicitly; (ii) compute the invariant subspace dimensions (block sizes after symmetrization); (iii) GO only if the largest block solves at a primal-dual gap ≤ the Rung 2 record (2.12e-08 lineage) on available hardware — else file NO-GO with the block sizes as the measured wall, appended to the C5060 scoping doc. If GO: solve, and record the number with the gap on the record, both directions of outcome pre-committed in writing (above 0.988: the fence stands, stated; below: the fence *could* lift, and promotion is explicitly a separate process with `attack_preflight`).
**The wall**: the scoping doc's own warning — the dim-32 solve already returned `optimal_inaccurate`; numerical hygiene (solver choice, scaling, gap reporting) is part of the deliverable, not an afterthought.
**Price class**: **$0**, bounded by the GO/NO-GO gate.
**Certifies as**: either the block-size NO-GO (a measured refinement of the C5060 scoping) or the symmetric-access ceiling with its gap — Cell 8's sharpest open question answered or its wall priced precisely.

### B2 THE HELM *(one GO/HOLD instrument where four weather dials now sit separately)*

**Trek frame**: the bridge has gauges scattered across four consoles; nobody can fly the ship watching all of them. Wire them into one helm annunciator.
**What's real underneath**: the campaign owns `qpu_weather.py` (live quiet-qubit map, readout weather, drift detector), the drift clock, same-depth sentinels (the window lottery is detectable in-run, never forecastable), and — once A1 lands — the census saying which dials matter for which flight class. These run today as separate habits.
**The composition**: one `helm.py`: pre-flight it evaluates weather + clock-phase + census-relevant constants for the declared flight class and emits GO/HOLD with reasons; in-run it packages the same-depth sentinel block as a standard insert and adjudicates it (the isotropy-gate lineage — in-flight adjudication that has actually fired and aborted a science block). **Positive controls, fault-injected**: a synthetic bad-weather snapshot must HOLD; a synthetic sentinel collapse must ABORT; a clean synthetic must GO — all three demonstrated in the tool's self-test before it guards anything real.
**The wall**: a controller that HOLDs too eagerly becomes a gate nobody obeys — the thresholds come *from the A1 census and the banked window-lottery statistics*, not from taste, and every threshold is written with its provenance.
**Price class**: **$0** to build and self-test; live shakedown rides along the next flight of any kind at zero marginal cost.
**Certifies as**: the three fault-injected demonstrations passing + first ride-along report.

### B3 THE SEED-STORM FENCE *(the gate that killed Cell 6, turned into a linter that kills such gates first)*

**Trek frame**: a hatch that latches or doesn't depending on which crewman compiles the order is not a hatch. Build the tester that shakes every hatch before anyone stakes a flight on it.
**What's real underneath**: H13 Cell 6 died because a premise gate cleared 0.95 by 0.0007 at one transpiler seed and failed at another (7 vs 9 2q gates) — *a gate that flips on a transpiler seed is not a gate*. That death is currently doctrine prose; it should be executable.
**The composition**: extend the gate-feasibility linter: transpile the design at K seeds (K frozen, e.g. 25), report the gate-count/depth distribution and, for every registered gate whose bar depends on compiled properties, whether PASS/FAIL is invariant across the distribution. Any flip = **NO-TEST at design time**, before a prereg freezes. **Positive control**: the banked Cell 6 circuit itself must flip (it is the known-answer input); a depth-robust known-good circuit must not.
**The wall**: seed-space is a proxy for compiler nondeterminism generally — the linter's claim is "unstable under the sampled seeds," never "stable under all compilation"; worded so in its output.
**Price class**: **$0**.
**Certifies as**: linter + self-test with both positive controls demonstrated; wired into the prereg checklist next to the vacuous-pass linter.

### B4 THE DECK PLATES *(board #117's placement-floor prescription, packaged so every absolute claim stands on measured ground)*

**Trek frame**: absolute readings depend on where on the deck you stand. Bolt the measuring stations to the plates and measure the plates first.
**What's real underneath**: board #117's ruling — relational claims certify, absolute claims need the **measured in-job placement floor** (K≥4 pinned placements), because the placement spread is 51× the noise model's sd and Cell 5 measured 0.324 sensitivity within one job. The prescription exists as doctrine; no reusable module implements it.
**The composition**: a prereg module + builder helper: declares the K pinned placements, co-flies them, computes the in-job floor, and grades the absolute quantity against it — one code path, KA-fenced, with the floor computation validated at $0 against the banked Cell 5 flights (known answer: sensitivity 0.324, the module must reproduce it from raw banked counts before it guards anything new).
**The wall**: K≥4 placements multiply circuit count — the module prices its own overhead in the prereg it emits, so a design sees the bill at freeze time, not at submit.
**Price class**: **$0** (validation is against banked data).
**Certifies as**: module reproducing Cell 5's 0.324 from raw counts + emitted-prereg template adopted into the flight kit.

### B5 THE ANCHOR COVENANT *(door (a)'s hard lesson, extracted so no future design re-learns it)*

**Trek frame**: two logbooks calibrated against different chronometers cannot be compared; the covenant is that every comparison carries its own clock.
**What's real underneath**: door (a)'s named failure surface — threshold sizing from an anchor that drifted **2.02×** between jobs while the same-job ratio held at 1.039 — produced R1–R6 (same-job frozen-formula τ, pessimistic-u sizing, grade-time validity gate). Those rules are door-a-specific text; the class of design they protect against is general.
**The composition**: extract the covenant into a reusable prereg fragment + a grade-time validity checker: any design comparing across jobs must either co-fly its anchor same-job or declare (and justify) a drift budget; the checker fails a grade whose anchor moved past the declared budget. **Positive controls from the banked record**: the 2.02× pair must FAIL the checker; the 1.039 same-job pair must PASS.
**The wall**: none of this changes door (a)'s own frozen R1–R6 — flight 3 flies as sealed; the covenant is for every design *after* it.
**Price class**: **$0**.
**Certifies as**: checker + both banked positive controls demonstrated; fragment adopted into the flight-kit template.
