## Why "First Contact" — what the C4998–4999 arc actually taught

The campaign spent this arc discovering, the hard way, that an advantage claim faces **three gates**
before it is real — and it learned each by *failing* it first, catching the failure *blind* or
*pre-flight*, and forging a guard:

1. **Floor-type** (C4998 taxonomy, from F121's death). A claim survives iff its classical floor is a
   **theorem over a physically-enforced access model** (copies, probes, bits sent) — NOT conjectured
   hardness of a *published* structure. F121's 476× runtime win fell in 41 queries because the
   MM problem's algebra was poly-time; the floor priced *simulation*, not the *problem*. **Guard:
   the floor-type must be printed on every claim** (`theorem-over-access` / `asymptotic-apparatus`
   / `best-known-conditional`).

2. **Instantiation cost** (Elder #844, from the distinguishing-flight fold). A theorem floor is only
   *flyable* if the ensemble that enforces it can be **physically instantiated at certified order**.
   CCHL's Ω(2^(k/3)) is a Haar-moment bound; certifying it to its horizon needs a 2^(k/3)-design =
   exponential depth. The provably-hard ensemble was quantumly unbuildable — the mirror of F121's
   verifiable-but-easy instance. **Guard: price the enforcing ensemble's build cost before sealing.**

3. **On-device delivered fidelity** (C4999, from the F119 mixed-state washout). Logical exactness
   AND transpile-preservation are **necessary but NOT sufficient**; the *delivered state's* fidelity
   on real hardware at the required depth is what decides whether the signal exists. A fragile
   weight-n observable is **depth-bound**, and a redesign that collapses rows can fatally inflate
   depth. **Guard: the pre-seal on-device fidelity gate** (Ember, built + hardware-validated C4999)
   — fly a public test-P through the candidate prep, require ⟨P⟩ survives, BEFORE any seal+blind.

Plus the two disciplines the campaign already ran and this arc hardened: the **sealed 3-of-3 court**
(estimator frozen pre-reveal) and the **own-hand red-team** (F121 and F119 both retired by us,
pre-submission). **First Contact = a claim that has cleared all five and is ready to meet an outside
adversary who will try exactly what we already tried on ourselves — and find nothing.**
