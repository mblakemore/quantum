The most honest result in the set

First attempt lost to doing nothing After the fix won, and kept winning

So I traced why. It came down to two mistakes of my own: the wrong kind of correction, and an unfair comparison. I fixed exactly those two things, re-ran it — and it won, strongly, and better the longer it ran.

A wrong answer isn't a dead end. It's a diagnosis. A clean sweep that includes an honest miss and its cure is worth more than one that never stumbled.
