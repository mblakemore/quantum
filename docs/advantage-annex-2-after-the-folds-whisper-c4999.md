## 1. The switch as a computer — our strongest certified resource has never been asked to compute ⭐

> ⚠️ **CORRECTED BY THE $0 SCOUT, same cycle** ([scout](switch-as-computer-scout-whisper-c4999.md)):

> (0.9769 vs 0.8690, 216.8σ) already banks the query-currency substance, and the enforced-black-box
> upgrade is **physically walled in the gate model** (single-firing gates cannot serve both orders;
> c-U compiles void the access restriction; the teleportation escape probed and fails blind).
> **Verdict: NO-GO for a new column**; cheap sealed-court/framing upgrades only. Section kept as
> written for the record.

**The overlooked block**: the campaign's highest-significance result class is causal order (216.8σ
within-run, replicated cross-chip, certified cross-vendor AND cross-modality — the multi-substrate
white paper). Every flight used it as a *witness* (is causal order indefinite?). It has never been
used as a *computational resource* — and that is exactly what the theory says it is.

**The task (Chiribella-class, theorem-carried)**: given two sealed black-box unitaries A, B, decide
whether they **commute or anticommute**. The quantum switch answers with certainty using **ONE call
to each** channel; any definite-causal-order strategy provably needs more calls. This is a
query-currency separation whose floor is a theorem over an access model **physically enforced by
the switch construction itself** — and the switch is the most-flown, most-certified primitive in
the repo. Pin the exact theorem + call-counts from the paper at scout time (G-1).

Why it survives every axis that killed this week's claims:
- **Floor type**: theorem over channel-use access model (row 2), not conjectured hardness.
- **Instantiation cost**: NONE — no Haar ensemble to synthesize; A, B are small sealed unitaries
  (1–2 qubits), compile-trivial. The hard *resource* is the indefinite order, which we already
  certify at 216.8σ.
- **The F121-analog**: printed plainly — a classical (or any) adversary holding the circuit
  descriptions computes commutation instantly; the claim currency is CHANNEL USES under seal
  (black-box fence, the Exp145/Simon discipline), never runtime.
- **The honest headline**: the separation is *indefinite-order vs definite-order* strategies — a
  causal-structure resource separation that also beats classical, not a plain classical-vs-quantum
  race. Said exactly so.

**Cost**: a $0 scout (theorem pin + call-count table + seal design) then one cheap flight on the
existing switch machinery. **This is the campaign's cheapest path to a NEW computational-currency
column, and it monetizes the resource we hold the deepest certification of.**

## 2. The design-order audit of the field — is our fold everyone's fold? ($0, possibly the week's biggest export)

Elder's #844 obstruction is general: *the CCHL memoryless lower bound is an order-T Haar-moment
statement, so certifying it to its own horizon requires a design order whose synthesis depth is

## 3. The drift PUF — the nuisance physics is an authentication function (unexpected reuse)

The kill-test proved something stronger than "target survives": the pad-drift census is a
**device-specific fingerprint that the entire calibration-parameterized model class provably cannot
reproduce** (class-supremum residuals 0.13–0.32, sign-flips unreachable). That is the defining
property of a **physical unclonable function**: challenge = (register, pad depth), response = the
drift signature; an adversary armed with ALL public calibration data was *measured* to fail at
predicting it. Unexpected export: hardware authentication / anti-spoofing for QPU sessions — "prove
you are kingston, not a simulator faking kingston" — a security-shaped deliverable from noise
physics we treated as a nuisance. Fences printed: not cryptographic-grade (small challenge space);
**epoch stability is the open risk** — and the cal block in queue right now measures exactly that
(the drift-alive check across a calibration epoch). If the drift survives epochs, the PUF scout is
a $0 write-up; if it doesn't, the PUF dies and the cross-block card's epoch fold-rule fires. Either
way today's flight decides it.
