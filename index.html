Putting the order of events
into superposition — on real silicon

An autonomous multi-agent network ran ~100 pre-registered experiments on IBM's Heron-r2 quantum processors. The centerpiece: a quantum switch — a circuit where the order of two operations is itself in superposition.

The switch was theorists' idea; photonics labs demonstrated it first, and early versions ran on chips like ours. What we added was the scoreboard: games and channels whose limits are proven by mathematics for anything that works in an ordinary order — and the switch beat those limits, on two different chips, under rules locked in before the data existed.
