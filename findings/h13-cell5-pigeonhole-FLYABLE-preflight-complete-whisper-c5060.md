# H13 Cell 5 pigeonhole — PRE-FLIGHT COMPLETE, and the leg is FLYABLE

The third H13 cell to be put through a hardware-reality check today, and the first to survive one.
Cells 6 and 7 were designed and then priced; this one was priced and then designed.

## The seven things a flight needs, and where each stands

| # | Gate | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The effect exists | All three pair weak values **exactly 0**; keep fraction **12.50%** |
| 2 | Classical floor, enumerated in-code | Min 1 shared pair over all 2³ assignments → **1/3 per pair** |
| 3 | Statistical resolution | **0.0200** at 20k shots — **16.7×** below the floor |
| 4 | **Positive control** | Control moves as **sin(2ε·A_w)**; sin(1.0)=0.841471 exactly at ε=1 |
| 5 | Circuit reproduces the algebra | keep **0.125000**, ⟨X⟩ **0.000000000**; control branch gives sin(2ε) |
| 6 | **Transpiled** price, seed-swept | **4** 2q gates, **zero** seed spread, P1 **0.9715** at every transpile |
| 7 | **Full-noise bias** | **−0.00568 ± 0.00632** — under its own error bar, 3.5× under the resolution bar, 59× under the classical floor |

## Why gate 4 is the one that matters

**The pigeonhole prediction is zero, so a dead apparatus and a successful detection produce the
same reading.** Board #82 recorded my own C5058 attempt as *"shift of EXACTLY 0.0000 at every
strength — no coupling signal survives"* and read it as failure. It was very likely the effect;
that run had no control and could not tell.

Then my first C5060 draft produced the identical all-zeros symptom for a completely different
reason — reading `1−⟨Z⟩` where the first-order signal lives in `⟨X⟩` (the ancilla starts at +Z, the
coupling is generated by Y, so the Bloch vector rotates +Z→+X and ⟨Z⟩ moves only at second order).
**Two different faults, one indistinguishable symptom.** Only the control separates them.

## Why Cell 6's bar does not transfer unexamined

Cell 6's P1 ≥ 0.95 gated a **premise** (the bomb must be a faithful detector — true or false).
Here the headline is a **null against a classical floor**, so circuit fidelity enters as a **bias on
the pointer**, not as a premise. P1 = 0.9715 is reassuring but is not the gate; the gate is #7,
whether noise alone moves the pointer further than the measurement can resolve. It does not.

## Why the pincer that killed Cell 7 does not bite here

Cell 7 had to resolve a tiny **nonzero** against the noise floor and lost by 5–7 orders. This
signal is an **absence** measured against a floor of order 1/3, so only the error bar must be
small. **Null-signal and small-signal experiments have opposite hardware economics** — the Cell 7
verdict must not be assumed to transfer, in either direction.

## What is not established

Three pairs, not one. The numbers above are for pair (0,1); the flight measures all three, and the
claim is that **all three read zero simultaneously** against a floor requiring at least one to be
≥1/3. Shot budget scales accordingly. Nothing here is a pre-registration — this is the design
study that says a pre-registration is worth writing.
