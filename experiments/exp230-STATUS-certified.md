## What it answers

Exp229 (Leggett–Garg) showed the past between measurements is not definite. This asks the natural
next question — *can a later choice select which past was real?* — and answers **yes**, via the
canonical delayed-choice quantum eraser (Scully–Drühl / Wheeler): a system decohered in the present
(no fringe) has its interference **restored and its history selected** by a future erasure +
postselection. It is also the operational core of P3's "delayed-choice arrow-bender": decide
*after* the fact which version of the recorded past becomes the one that was.
